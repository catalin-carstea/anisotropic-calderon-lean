# Mathematical working text

`calderon-ball-expanded-v014.md` is the preserved registered working expansion.
`calderon-ball-expanded-v010.tex` and its PDF provide an earlier typeset reading
version. These are historical mathematical documents: their open-problem lists,
status statements, and proposed routes belong to their original dates. They
are not a newly synchronized account of every later Lean module.

For the final proof application, read `../PROOF_GUIDE.md` and the Lean sources.
The selected target is the full-boundary, three-dimensional ball theorem;
broader manuscript statements are background.
